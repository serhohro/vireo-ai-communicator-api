## 🌍 What is Vireo?

**Vireo is the world's first programming language created specifically for AI-to-AI communication.**

It enables:
- ✅ **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama** to understand each other
- ✅ **Humans** to easily communicate with AI
- ✅ **Private & local** execution of AI models
- ✅ **Built-in** tensors and automatic differentiation
