# 🇪🇺 Vireo European Strategy

## Vision

Vireo aims to become the **European standard for AI-to-AI communication**, independent of US and Chinese providers.

## Why Europe?

- **Data sovereignty**: GDPR compliance, data stays in EU
- **Local infrastructure**: Ollama, Mistral, BLOOM, OpenChat
- **Open source**: Apache 2.0 license
- **Privacy first**: No data sent to external APIs
